PocketLinux experimental libhybris runtime (arm64 / glibc guests)

Source: prebuilt tree packed by tawc v1
  https://github.com/wmww/tawc  (scripts/build-libhybris.sh)
  https://github.com/wmww/libhybris  (stock-Android fork of libhybris/libhybris)

Asset: arm64-v8a.tar  (extracted at runtime to filesDir/libhybris)

This is a glibc aarch64 build. Alpine/musl guests cannot load it.
